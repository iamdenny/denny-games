Denny Games adaptations
The runtime is self-hosted in an opaque-origin iframe with a no-network CSP.
The host adds a checked postMessage bridge, pauses requestAnimationFrame while
the game is paused, forwards supported gamepad actions to the upstream keyboard
controls, localizes the game and common controls in Korean, removes the
upstream home/back and fullscreen exits, suppresses the first-run tutorial,
disables share links and in-game currency rewards, and adapts the page to the
Denny Games player. Original cover art is by Denny Games. The imported game
engine and upstream assets are not represented as Denny Games originals.
