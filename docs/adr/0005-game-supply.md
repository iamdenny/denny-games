# Game supply and rights

## Status

Accepted — 2026-10-11.

## Context

English/Korean browser arcade with free static hosting, expandable game inventory and advertising requirements. Later user decisions take precedence over the research draft.

## Decision

Implement the two PRD games and an adaptation of upstream MIT 2048 with original UI/assets. Admit other researched games only after per-file rights review. Preserve source commit and upstream notice.

## Consequences

Three initial titles; 294 research entries are not automatically published or certified.

## Verification

Owning modules and release checks are mapped in [PRD](../product/PRD.md) and [testing](../testing.md).
